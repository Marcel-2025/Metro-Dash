Privacy Policy (iOS)

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Applies to “Metro Dash” for iPhone (on the App Store: “Metro Dash – Endless Runner”). The German version is Datenschutzerklärung iOS.

Marcel Single

2. Summary

Metro Dash is an offline game. It needs no account and the developer runs no server. The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs and no third-party crash reporting. No personal data is sent to the developer or to third parties.

3. Game progress and settings

High score, coins, EXP and level, unlocked characters, boards and upgrades, inventory, mission progress and settings (music, sound effects) are stored only in the app’s local storage on your iPhone, solely so you can continue your game. They may be included in your iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), which are managed by Apple or by you.

4. In-app purchases

Metro Dash offers optional in-app purchases (e.g. coin packs, characters, boards). Purchase and payment are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Metro Dash receives no payment or account data, only the information from iOS which product was bought, so it can be unlocked in the game. “Restore purchases” in the game’s settings asks Apple which permanent purchases belong to your Apple Account. Apple’s processing is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

Metro Dash requests no permissions: no access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or notifications.

6. App Store

When you download or update Metro Dash from the App Store, Apple processes data as the platform operator under its own responsibility. If you share analytics with app developers (iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple provides the developer with anonymised crash reports. You can turn this off there at any time.

7. Children

Metro Dash is not directed specifically at children. As the app collects no personal data, no data is collected from children either. In-app purchases can be blocked or require parental approval via iOS Screen Time.

8. Retention and deletion

Your progress stays on your device until you delete the app, at which point iOS removes all app data. The developer keeps no server-side copy. Permanent purchases can be restored after reinstalling via “Restore purchases”.

9. Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub processes technically necessary connection data such as your IP address. The website sets no cookies and uses no tracking. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since Metro Dash stores your data only locally, the developer generally holds no data about you.

11. Changes

This policy will be updated if features or services change, for example if advertising or online features are added in future. The version published here applies.
